### 💫 About Me

I am a **Computer Science Engineering student at PES University (EC Campus)** with a deep passion for **Systems Programming, Low-Level Software, and Cloud Infrastructure**. 

I thrive on understanding the inner workings of computers—managing manual memory, minimizing latency, reasoning about concurrency, and architecting distributed systems. Rather than focusing on surface-level frontend code, my mission is to engineer high-throughput, resilient backend and systems-level software that scales.

- 🔭 **Current Focus:** Deep-diving into Operating System internals, custom shell implementations (`GoShell`), and high-performance concurrency in Go.
- ⚙️ **Core Philosophy:** Understand the abstractions beneath the compiler—memory layouts, syscalls, network packet flows, and hardware boundaries.
- 🌐 **Networking & Cloud:** Designing flow rules and switch-controller dynamics in Software-Defined Networks (SDN) and automated mesh networks (Tailscale).
- 💬 **Ask Me About:** C, C++, Go, OS architecture, Linux systems programming, audio DSP with JUCE, and SDN with Mininet/OpenFlow.
- ⚡ **Fun Fact:** When I'm not writing low-level code, you can find me experimenting with real-time audio plugins or exploring game engine architectures.
